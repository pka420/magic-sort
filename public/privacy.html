Privacy Policy

Magic Sort — https://magic-sort.from-delhi.net/ — Last updated: 29 August 2026

This policy describes what Magic Sort collects when you play at https://magic-sort.from-delhi.net/, why we collect it, and your choices. Magic Sort is a hobby puzzle game; we collect the minimum needed to run accounts and leaderboards and to fund hosting via advertising.

1. Who we are

Magic Sort is operated as a personal project. For privacy enquiries contact us via the email shown in our AdSense account or via the GitHub repository linked from the game. We are the data controller for account data.

2. What we collect

  • Account info you provide: username, email address, and a password hash (we never store raw passwords). If you use “Sign in with Google” we receive your Google ID, email, and profile name from Google.
  • Game data: leaderboard scores per level, username shown publicly on leaderboards, and whether your email is verified.
  • Local save: your campaign progress and banked score are kept in your browser’s localStorage (and a signed opaque save). We do not see it until you submit a score.
  • Technical logs: standard server logs (IP address, user-agent, request time) retained briefly for security and debugging.
  • Cookies & similar: a bearer token in localStorage to keep you signed in; AdSense and its partners may set cookies or use local storage for ads personalisation (see §4).

3. How we use it

  • To create and authenticate your account, verify email, reset passwords, and assign your leaderboard entries.
  • To keep you signed in and to recover your session after a reload.
  • To prevent abuse (rate limiting, verifying saves, moderating usernames).
  • To serve and measure ads (AdSense) where you have consented where required by law.

Legal bases: performance of the account contract, legitimate interests (security, running leaderboards), and consent for advertising cookies where applicable.

4. Advertising — Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-8317099814214393). Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to serve and personalize ads, measure performance, and — if you consent — show personalized ads based on your browsing. See How Google uses data when you use partners’ sites and Google Privacy Policy.

You can control personalised ads at adssettings.google.com and via your browser’s cookie settings. In the EEA/UK/Switzerland we will request consent for non-essential cookies before AdSense sets them; you may withdraw consent at any time by clearing site data.

Our ads.txt at /ads.txt lists authorized sellers: google.com, pub-8317099814214393, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share only with:

  • Google for authentication (if you choose it) and advertising.
  • Email provider to send verification and password-reset messages.
  • Hosting provider (VPS/nginx and any CDN in front of it) that processes requests on our behalf.

6. Retention

We keep account and score data until you ask us to delete your account. Verification and reset tokens expire (reset links: 15 minutes) and are cleared after use. Server logs are rotated and kept only as long as needed for security (typically < 30 days). Local saves remain in your browser until you clear site data.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise them, or to delete your account and leaderboard entries, contact us using the address associated with the site. If you are in the EEA/UK you may also complain to your supervisory authority.

8. Children

Magic Sort is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are a parent and believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Passwords are stored as hashes; tokens are signed and expire. No method is 100% secure, but we apply reasonable technical measures and limit retention.

10. International transfers

Your data may be processed on servers outside your country (e.g., where Google or our host operates), with safeguards as provided by those providers.

11. Changes

We will update this page and change the “Last updated” date when the policy changes. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Summary for account sign-up: by creating an account you confirm you have read and agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service. You may use the game without an account; an account is only needed for leaderboards.
