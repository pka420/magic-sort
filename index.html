Magic Sort

Magic Sort is a free liquid-sorting puzzle in the browser. Coloured elixirs sit stacked in glass flasks. Pour them until every flask holds a single pure colour. There is nothing to install: open the page and play.

How to play

Tap a flask to pick it up, then tap another to pour. A pour is legal when the target is empty, or its top layer matches what you are pouring, and it has room. The whole unbroken run of the top colour moves at once. Sort every flask to finish the level. Solving a level opens the next.

Fifty handcrafted levels make up the campaign. Early levels teach the pour with spare flasks; later ones take the spare room away, mix flask heights, and scatter free layers so there is nowhere to dump a whole run. Eight colours are in play by the end, on boards of nine flasks.

Scoring

Each level pays more than the one before it. The first is worth 1,000 points and the fiftieth 50,000; a flawless campaign totals 1,275,000. Extra pours past the shortest solution cut the payout. Restarting a level costs a tenth of that level from your banked score. Walking back to level one keeps what you have earned but charges the campaign behind you. If you cannot pay, the run ends.
